# Request to the organiser: player artwork for the Six Kings Slam Predictor

Ready to paste into an email.

---

For the Six Kings Slam Predictor we'd like to use your official 2026 player artwork rather than generic
photos. Could you send the following for the six players: Carlos Alcaraz, Novak Djokovic,
Jannik Sinner, Alexander Zverev, Alex de Minaur and Taylor Fritz.

**Per player**

- One portrait in your Six Kings Slam style, 3:4 (upright), at least 900 × 1200 px, JPG, PNG or WebP,
  under 5 MB. A transparent cut-out (PNG) is welcome too; we place it on our own background.
- The same crop and framing for all six, so they line up side by side.

**Rights**

- Written confirmation that we may show them in the Predictor (web app, its share images and the
  sign-in emails) from launch on 17 Oct until 30 days after the final, 24 Oct 2026.
- Any credit line or restriction we must show (photographer, agency, "© Six Kings Slam").

**Also useful, if you have them**

- The official schedule (start time of each match), to replace our estimate of 19:30 and about 20:40
  Riyadh time each night.
- Your logo files (SVG) and brand colours and fonts.

We host the images ourselves; nothing is linked from another site. Until they arrive, the game shows
each player's name and initials.
